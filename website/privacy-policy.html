Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Annapurna Vegetables and Fruits respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is used when you browse our store or place an order.

Information we collect

When you place an order, we may collect your name, mobile number, delivery address, order details and payment status. Payment credentials such as card numbers, UPI PINs and banking passwords are handled by the payment provider; we do not ask you to share your UPI PIN or banking password with the store.

How we use information

We use order and contact information to confirm orders, arrange delivery or collection, provide customer support, maintain transaction records and comply with applicable legal requirements.

Sharing

We share only the information needed with service providers involved in payment processing, order fulfilment, delivery and technical hosting, or where required by law.

Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect order information and retain it only as needed for business, legal and accounting purposes.

Your choices

For questions or requests about your information, contact the store using the business contact details published on this website.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when our services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page.
