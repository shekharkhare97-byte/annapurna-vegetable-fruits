Shipping, Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Delivery

Delivery is available only in serviceable areas. Estimated delivery timing and any delivery charge are communicated when the order is confirmed. Fresh produce availability may vary.

Cancellation

Please contact the store as soon as possible to request cancellation. Cancellation may not be possible once the order has been prepared or dispatched. The store will confirm whether a cancellation can be accepted.

Damaged, missing or incorrect items

If an item is damaged, missing or different from what was ordered, contact the store promptly with your order details so the issue can be reviewed and an appropriate replacement or refund can be arranged where applicable.

Refunds

Approved refunds for online payments are initiated to the original payment method through the payment provider. The time taken for the refund to appear depends on the payment provider and the customer's bank. Cash-on-delivery orders are not charged online; any adjustment is handled by the store.

Contact

For delivery, cancellation or refund support, contact the store using the business contact details published on this website.
